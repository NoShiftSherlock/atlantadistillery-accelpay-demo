Our story

Crafted from passion

What started in a Marietta garage with founders Mark and Liz Deno making small-batch hard cider grew into Georgia’s top-selling craft cider brand, and then a line of apple-based spirits.

Every drop is made on-site at the family-owned cidery and distillery in Marietta: all-natural, gluten-free and bottled by hand.

How this works

This is Atlanta Distillery's own Shopify store with the AccelPay Shopify app installed in place of the third-party buy button. The cart and checkout are Shopify. AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing each order to a licensed retailer.

01Shopify cartShoppers add to cart on this site, in the native Shopify cart. No third-party cart and no redirect.
02Shopify checkoutThe one-page Shopify checkout with Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and card payments by AccelPay.
03Licensed retailerThe shipping address picks a licensed retailer that sells and ships the order. Retailers set the final retail price.
04Your customer dataEvery order and customer record lands in Atlanta Distillery's Shopify admin. You own the customer.
For the Atlanta Distillery team

Swap the buy button for your own Shopify checkout

Today each product page loads a third-party buy button that opens its own cart. With the AccelPay Shopify app, Add to cart uses your Shopify cart and checkout, AccelPay takes over payments, taxes and order routing to licensed retailers, and the customer data stays in your Shopify admin.

  • Ships to 40+ states through 3,500+ licensed retail locations
  • Instant delivery in about 30 minutes through Gopuff, plus DoorDash from local retailers, where a retailer stocks the bottle
  • Live in about one week from kickoff, with no per-order charge to the brand
  • A ReserveBar.com listing comes with it

Brands that switch their white-label store to our plugins (the AccelPay or LiquidCommerce cart, or our native Shopify integration) automatically receive advertising credit on ReserveBar.com and Minibar.

Reordering

Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.
